================================================================================
RECOMMENDED SPACING SCALE
================================================================================

Base Unit: 8px (industry standard)

Scale (multiples of 4-8):
  4px  - Micro spacing (rare)
  8px  - Tight (rare)
  12px - Compact (occasional)
  16px - DEFAULT (most common)
  24px - Secondary (very common)
  32px - Major (common)
  48px - Large (occasional)
  64px - Extra large (digibord)

Pattern: 1x to 1.5x to 2x to 3x to 4x (proportional harmony)

This is:
  + Web standard (Bootstrap, Material Design)
  + Proportional (recognizable rhythm)
  + Easy to remember
  + Accessible (scales with content)
